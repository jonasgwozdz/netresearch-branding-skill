How it works

Three steps from account to a course you can test.

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Enter your name and work email. After you confirm, you land in your own course area.

  2. 2

    Upload the course ZIP

    Add your course package as a ZIP file. Netresearch receives it and prepares the course.

  3. 3

    Test the prepared course

    Open the prepared course in your browser and try it out before you use it.

Good to know

Server in Germany

Your uploaded course files are stored on a server located in Germany.

No payment details needed

Creating an account does not ask for a credit card or any other payment information.

Sign-in by email link

There is no password to remember. Each time you sign in, we send a link to your email.
